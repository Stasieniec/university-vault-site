## Concepts

The **[[Concepts/|Concepts]]** folder is a flat wiki of about 275 interconnected notes spanning all the courses. Each concept links to the lectures where it appears and to related concepts. Use the **graph view** or **backlinks** to explore connections.

## How to use these notes

- **Flashcards**: open a course's flashcards page, pick the whole course or single lectures, and press Study
- **Search** (Ctrl/Cmd+K) to find any topic
- **Graph view** to explore how concepts connect
- **Backlinks** at the bottom of each page show what links here
- All formulas are in LaTeX, all definitions use callouts
