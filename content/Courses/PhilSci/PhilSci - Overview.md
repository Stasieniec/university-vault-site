## Objectives

1. Acquire knowledge of central debates in contemporary philosophy of science at master's level
2. Skills: handle primary literature, present a text (possibly collaboratively), debate at academic level, concisely represent a text in short writing
3. Attitude: critically reflect upon scientific knowledge from a meta-perspective; approach texts analytically

## Course Content

1. **Theory vs Evidence:** Logical empiricism, induction, prediction, evidence
2. **Kuhn:** Philosophy of science, scientific revolutions
3. **Scientific Realism Debate:** No-miracles argument, constructive empiricism, pessimistic meta-induction, inference to the best explanation, structural realism
4. **Duhem-Quine Thesis:** Underdetermination
5. **Explanation and Understanding**
6. **Laws of Nature**
